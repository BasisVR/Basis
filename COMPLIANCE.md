# Complying with the Basis license

This guide exists because we've encountered a couple of instances of users
of the Basis framework stripping or otherwise omitting the Basis license
notice. We take license compliance seriously.

Basis is released under the MIT License. You may use, copy, modify, merge,
publish, distribute, sublicense, and sell it, including in closed-source and
commercial projects. In exchange, the license imposes one condition. It is
easy to fulfill, but it is NOT optional:

**Every copy you distribute must include the Basis copyright notice and
license text.** This applies to builds of your client, not just source code.
"We forgot" is not an exemption; it is still a violation.

This applies to any substantial portion of Basis, not just the full
framework: if you extract, port, or adapt a component, system, or script from
Basis, include the Basis notice (and any third-party notices that component
depends on) just as a full client would. If you're unsure whether what
you've taken counts, include the notice.

## How to comply

- **Keep the "Third-Party Licenses" screen** in your client, reachable from
  normal UI (settings, about, or credits). If you rebuild or reskin the UI,
  carry it over. This is what we expect of every Basis-based client.
- **Keep third-party notices intact.** Basis bundles libraries whose licenses
  also require attribution, and their notices are part of that screen. If
  you add dependencies, add their notices too.
- **If you redistribute source,** keep `LICENSE` at the repository root and
  leave existing copyright headers intact.
- **You may add your own copyright notice** for your changes. You may not
  remove ours.

## Non-compliance

The rights MIT grants you are conditional on preserving the notice. If you
distribute Basis without it, you are not licensed to distribute it at all.
That is copyright infringement, no matter how heavily you've modified Basis
or how the notice went missing.

When we find a non-compliant distribution, we will contact you and expect
the notice restored in a prompt update.

Please be advised that omitting copyright notices can entitle not just Basis
but the authors of any bundled library that requires attribution to file
takedown notices against your project. Stripping license notices is not a
gray area.
